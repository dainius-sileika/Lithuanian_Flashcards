# Pilot: 24 bubble-free renders + 10 extras (Fable, 2026-10-17)

Owner decisions applied: single scenes, comics withdrawn; question-word cards keep a picture; 374 galvoti redrawn without the thought bubble; English caption in the bottom band, section name in English in the top band.

Purpose of the pilot: prove that a picture with no bubble and no pictogram, under an English caption, (1) shows the right situation, (2) cannot be mistaken for its neighbour in the deck, (3) contains no stray lettering or stray speech act, and (4) still looks like a GO plate at phone size. The 24 are the hardest sets: the five greetings (time-of-day and register must carry them), thanks/apology/politeness (gesture must not cross over), the two good-luck cards, the price card, and all 12 two-panel cards.

## What the owner judges, per card (one line each on the review page)

1. **Situation** — "With the English caption, does the picture show the moment this phrase is said?" yes / nearly / no.
2. **Neighbour test** — "Shown without the caption, could I take this for the neighbour listed in the last column?" The cue in the sixth column must be visible on a phone without zooming. yes = fail.
3. **Stray act** — any bubble, floating symbol, lettering, or a gesture that belongs to another phrase (handshake, wave, bowed head, thumb-up, pointing) that the phrase does not call for. Any = re-roll.
4. **Register** — clothes, age and setting fit tu/jūs and the period (1981, interwar/partisan cues only where the staging says so).
5. **Plate** — the frame, cream paper and figure scale still read as a GO plate with the drawn bands trimmed and the caption band added by the builder.
6. For the two-panel cards: same two people, same scale, both panels equally legible at phone width; the right panel's action answers the left panel's ask.
7. For the question words: accept that *kodėl, kaip, koks, kuris* are situations, not objects. The question is only whether the scene is the one in which the word is used, not whether the word could be guessed.
8. For 374 galvoti: does the chin-in-hand reading survive without the bubble, and is it distinct from *to remember* / *to read*?

Scoring: a card passes with Situation yes/nearly, Neighbour no, Stray none, Register ok, Plate ok. Three or more failures in one set (e.g. greetings) means the set's staging rule is wrong, not the render — stop and re-stage before spending on the remaining 133.

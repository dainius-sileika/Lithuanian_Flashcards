# Historical likenesses for person, profession and precedent-object cards

Plan only (2026-10-17). Nothing applied.

## 0. The tension the owner should see first

The locked style spec says: "Figures are types, not individuals. Anonymous everypersons ... The style is about the coordinated many performing a procedure, never the exalted one" (GO_STYLE_SPEC.md, "Two structural commitments"). Both production leads in go_generator.py open with "People are ordinary Lithuanians of 1981, anonymous types". A recognisable Maironis or Darius on a card is a break from that lock.

Two ways to reconcile it, and this plan proposes the second:

1. Portrait likeness: ask the model for the person's face. Breaks the lock, is unreliable at card scale (see feasibility.md), and raises a real-person problem for a public deck.
2. Evocation: keep the figure an anonymous type, but give the card the figure's world: the orange monoplane with aerofoil struts, the headscarf and black dress at a writing table, the long white beard and physician's bag, the 1939 hall with its steel arches. Lithuanians who know the reference see it; everyone else sees a correct card. This keeps the "many, not the exalted one" rule and the deadpan register.

Where a figure is proposed below, "likeness" means route 2 unless the owner chooses route 1 after the test.

## 1. Vetting method

Each candidate was checked in Visuotinė lietuvių enciklopedija (vle.lt) and, where relevant, the Genocide and Resistance Research Centre's published positions (via LRT) and other named sources. Questions asked: did the person hold office in, take honours from, or make propaganda for the Soviet regime (1940–41, 1944–90)? Did they collaborate with the German occupation (1941–44)? Is there an authoritarian-rule or other public-reputation issue? Is the person living?

Verdict scale: **clear** (no Soviet or Nazi service; dead before 1940 or a victim/émigré), **caution** (nothing disqualifying, but a reputational question the owner should know), **exclude** (served, profited from, or made propaganda for the Soviet machine, or collaborated with the Nazis).

Only facts read in the cited source are stated. "Not read" means I did not open a source and make no claim.

## 3. Card-by-card proposals (evocation route)

### 012 lėktuvas (plane) — Lituanica
- Current: "a passenger airplane", generic, no setting.
- Proposal: the main subject becomes a single-engine high-wing monoplane painted orange, with wide aerofoil-shaped struts bracing the wing to the fuselage, a radial engine with a ring cowl, fixed undercarriage with two spoked wheels, standing on a grass field before a wooden hangar; two men in leather flying jackets and caps beside it, one checking the engine (no faces turned to the viewer). No lettering (the real plane carried its name and sponsor scrolls; the deck's no-text rule drops them).
- Insets: the plane in level flight over the sea; the cockpit from outside; the propeller.
- Source for the look: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lituanica (Bellanca CH-300 Pacemaker, painted orange, distinctive wing struts, 365 hp Wright Whirlwind radial, 15–17 July 1933).
- Also: an inset on 029 oro uostas and the hall of A2-0652 muziejus can carry the same orange monoplane.

### 123 autorius (author, man + woman) — Binkis and Žemaitė
- Current: two authors at typewriters at a shared desk, generic.
- Proposal: the woman author is an older woman in a dark dress with a plain white headscarf tied under the chin, writing with a pen on loose sheets (Žemaitė's universally known image; she wrote by hand). The man is a lean clean-shaven man in his forties with a high forehead and swept-back hair, in a dark suit without a tie, at a typewriter (Binkis). Keep the shared desk and bookshelves.
- Why: Lithuanian learners recognise the headscarf-and-pen figure as Žemaitė on sight; the man reads as "a writer" to everyone and as Binkis to nobody who is not told — that is fine.
- Note: "Žemaitė in a headscarf" is from her photographs and the 1-litas banknote image, not from the VLE text, which does not describe her appearance.

### A2-0792 rašytojas (writer, single man) — Vaižgantas (first), Sruoga or Binkis (alternates)
- Current: a writer at a desk hammering a typewriter, pipe, cat on the sill.
- Vaižgantas version: a stout older man with a round face and round spectacles, in a black cassock with a white collar, writing by hand at a cluttered desk with newspapers and proofs, a window onto a church tower beside the river; the caption carries "writer" so the cassock does not mislead. Risk: the picture leans toward "priest" (kunigas, card 117); the English caption mitigates but the image-first rule prefers an unambiguous figure.
- Sruoga version: a tall gaunt man with a long face and a shock of dark hair, in a worn jacket, writing in a plain room; a pine forest through the window (Dievų miškas). Unambiguous as "writer", but the reference will not land without the face.
- Recommendation: Sruoga or Binkis for A2-0792 (the single-writer card must read as writer first), Vaižgantas only if the owner accepts a cassock on a writer card.

### 113 gydytojas (doctor, man + woman) — Basanavičius for the man (optional)
- Proposal: the man doctor is elderly with a very long full white beard and a high forehead, in a dark frock coat under his white coat, a physician's bag and stethoscope; the woman doctor as now. The long beard is the one Lithuanian "face" that survives any drawing scale.
- Risk: he reads as "an old doctor"; the beard could tip a learner toward senelis (grandfather, card 091). The white coat and stethoscope hold the card.
- Alternates with the same profession: Kudirka (moustache, Varpas) or Grinius (physician-president) — weaker visually.

### 122 menininkas (artist, man + woman) — Čiurlionis (optional)
- Proposal: the man artist is slim with dark swept-back hair and a dark moustache, painting a canvas of a stylised sea or sun with concentric rays (a Čiurlionis-like motif) at his easel; Užupis setting as assigned. Low priority: the motif is the only carrier and insets must not confuse the card.

### A2-0782 inžinierius (engineer) — Gustaitis (optional)
- Proposal: replace "a large technical drawing of a bridge" with a plan-and-elevation drawing of a small low-wing monoplane on the drawing board, a model aircraft on the desk; the engineer in a plain uniform jacket without insignia. Reads as "engineer" to all, as the ANBO designer to those who know.

### 138 komanda, A2-1297 krepšinis — the 1939 champions, no faces
- Proposal: players in plain white sleeveless shirts and white shorts with a diagonal tricolour sash or band across the chest, canvas boots, in the Kaunas Sports Hall with its steel arches overhead; one very tall centre among them (Lubinas without his face). The kit colours are unverified: VLE describes no uniform. The executor should check a 1939 team photograph before fixing colours; if unverified, plain white with a tricolour band is a safe counterfactual kit.
- Source for the hall and the title: https://www.vle.lt/straipsnis/lietuvos-krepsinis/ ; https://www.vle.lt/straipsnis/kauno-sporto-hale/

### A2-1769 paminklas — the Freedom Statue instead of the Soviet-era seated poet
- See real_places.md. A winged figure striding on a tall plinth with broken chains, in a museum garden with a bell tower behind.

### Cards where no figure is proposed, and why
- 117 kunigas: Maironis and Vaižgantas were priests, but their fame is as writers; a priest card does not need a famous face.
- 110 mokytojas: Jablonskis is the teacher-of-the-nation, but a bearded elderly man in a two-teacher classroom adds nothing a learner can use.
- 125 žurnalistas: Kudirka founded Varpas; a street reporter with a microphone is a different image.
- 126 aktorius, 124 vadovas, 116 sekretorius, 115 padavėjas, A2-0791 muzikantas, A2-1236 dainininkas (Petrauskas excluded), A2-0794 mokslininkas, A2-1416 šokėjas: generic.
- 095/096 karalius/karalienė: Mindaugas is the only crowned king; no authentic likeness exists; the storybook figure is right.
- A2-1370 teisėjas, 112 advokatas: the Supreme Tribunal's first chairman Antanas Kriščiukaitis (d. 1933) is a possible precedent but not vetted and not recognisable; the Kaunas courtroom does the work.

If the owner approves only a few: (1) 012 Lituanica, (2) 123 Žemaitė + Binkis, (3) A2-1297 / A2-0656 Kaunas Sports Hall kit, (4) A2-1769 Freedom Statue, (5) 113 Basanavičius, (6) A2-0792 writer, (7) A2-0782 Gustaitis, (8) 122 Čiurlionis.

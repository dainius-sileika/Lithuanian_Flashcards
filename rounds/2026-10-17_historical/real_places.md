# Real places for place, building and institution cards

Plan only (2026-10-17). Nothing here has been applied to card_notes.json, a2_staging.json or settings_assignments.csv.

Rule of thumb used: a building must plausibly stand and be in use in a free Lithuania of 1981. Interwar Kaunas (the temporary capital, 1919–40) is preferred because it is the last period in which Lithuania built its own state institutions; older buildings are fine; Soviet-era buildings are used only where the existing bank already uses them (Lazdynai, the TV tower, the 1953 Kaunas station) and nothing older fits.

Each entry gives: the card(s), the place, one line of what it looks like so a prompt can describe it without naming it (the generator's location bank already works this way: `prompt_phrase` describes, `name` is optional), the 1981 counterfactual note, and sources. "Visual, unverified" marks a visual detail taken from photographs or memory that I could not confirm in a text source; the executor should check it against a photograph before writing the prompt.

Existing assignments (style_lab/research/settings_assignments.csv, 82 located cards) are kept unless marked **flag**.

## A. New real-place proposals

### 035 teismas (court) and A2-1370 teisėjas (judge), A2-1347 seimas, 112 advokatas (courtroom)
- Place: Palace of the Ministry of Justice and the Seimas, Kaunas (E. Ožeškienės g. 12 / L. Sapiegos g.), architect Edmundas Alfonsas Frykas, built 1925–28/30; housed the Ministry of Justice, the Supreme Tribunal and, from 1936, the Seimas; today the Kaunas State Philharmonic.
- Look: a heavy three-storey neoclassical corner block with a ground-floor arcade of round arches at the entrance, tall windows above, a plain cornice and a low hipped roof; the entrance arcade once bore a Latin motto (no lettering on the card). Inside: a lobby with marble columns whose capitals carry carved folk ornament; a tall hall lit from above by skylights (the former Seimas hall). Visual, unverified: the arcade count and roof form.
- Card use: 035 courtroom interior with an inset of the arcaded front; A2-1370 the same courtroom; A2-1347 the skylit hall with curved benches; 112 the lawyers' table in that courtroom.
- 1981 note: in a free Lithuania the Supreme Tribunal would still sit here (or in Vilnius); either way the building stands. The real Seimas in 1981 would more likely sit in Vilnius; the Vilnius Supreme Council building was only under construction in 1980–82 and reads as Soviet, so the Kaunas hall is the safer picture.
- **Flag:** A2-1347's staging names "the Vytis on the wall", which the locked flag-only rule forbids (heraldry garbles). Replace with a large tricolour.
- Sources: https://www.vle.lt/straipsnis/kauno-architektura/ ("Seimo ir Teisingumo ministerijos rūmai, 1930, architektas E. A. Frykas ... dabar Kauno filharmonija"); https://architectuul.com/architecture/the-ministry-of-justice-and-the-parliament-building (skylit hall, folk-carved column capitals, Parliament from 1936); https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaunas_State_Philharmonic (built 1925–28, motto over the entrance, Neumann–Sass trial 1934–35).

### A2-0649 paštas (post office), A2-1326 paštininkas (inset), A2-1351 siuntinys (optional)
- Place: Kaunas Central Post Office, Laisvės al. 102, architect Feliksas Vizbaras, 1930–32. UNESCO-listed (Modernist Kaunas, 2023).
- Look: a smooth pale rendered modernist block with a flat roof, very large windows set in carved wooden frames, cement cornices with folk motifs, an electric clock on the front; inside, a wide tall hall whose ceramic floor is laid in a pattern like a woven linen towel (national weave), brass-railed counters. Visual, unverified: the stepped central bay.
- 1981 note: still the main post office of Kaunas in any 1981.
- Sources: https://kaunas2022.eu/modernizmasateiciai/en/kauno-centrinis-pastas/index.html (flat roof, large windows, electric clock, floor "like a hand-made traditional linen towel"); https://kaunasin.lt/en/what-to-see/former-kaunas-central-post-office-palace/ (wood-carved window rims, cement cornices, ceramic floor in national weave); https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaunas_Central_Post_Office (1930–32, brick, UNESCO 2023); https://www.vle.lt/straipsnis/kauno-architektura/.

### 055 bankas (bank) and A2-0667 banko skyrius (bank branch)
- Place: Bank of Lithuania building, Kaunas, Maironio g. 25 / K. Donelaičio g. 85, architect Mykolas Songaila, 1924–28. Still the Bank of Lithuania today.
- Look: a monumental neoclassical corner building of three storeys in pale stone-coloured render with a columned entrance; inside, a lobby in black, yellow and brown marble, black marble Ionic columns, grey granite stairs, coffered ceilings with rosettes, a great operations hall under a triangular glass skylight, grey geometric floor tiles, heavy chandeliers. Visual, unverified: exact column order on the façade.
- 1981 note: the central bank of a free Lithuania, or its Kaunas branch; nothing to change.
- Existing assignment: 055 is "kaunas_modernism, bank with columned entrance in the interwar banking quarter" — this is a refinement, not an override.
- Sources: https://autc.lt/en/architecture-object/?id=661 (address, Songaila, completed 1928, marble lobby, Ionic columns, skylight, tiles, chandeliers); https://www.vle.lt/straipsnis/kauno-architektura/ ("Lietuvos banko pastatas, 1928, architektas M. Songaila").

### A2-0652 muziejus (museum)
- Place: Vytautas the Great War Museum with the M. K. Čiurlionis Art Museum, Kaunas, Vienybės square, architects V. Dubeneckis and K. Reisonas, engineer K. Krikščiukaitis, opened 16 February 1936.
- Look: a long low pale block in a mix of classicism and early functionalism, with a tall square bell tower (carillon, 35 bells in 1935) rising at one end; a garden in front with the Freedom Statue (an angel on a tall plinth, Zikaras 1928) and an eternal flame; inside, the orange Lituanica aircraft hanging in a hall, old cannons, and in the art wing Čiurlionis's paintings and amber.
- 1981 note: in a free Lithuania the garden statues (removed by the Soviets in the 1950s) stand, the Freedom Statue included — a legitimate counterfactual marker, like the cathedral statues already used.
- Sources: https://www.vle.lt/straipsnis/kauno-architektura/ (1936, architects, "klasicizmo ir modernizmo derinys"); https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vytautas_the_Great_War_Museum (Art Deco/early functionalism, carillon 1935, Lituanica on display, eternal flame); https://www.vle.lt/straipsnis/juozas-zikaras/ (Laisvė statue, 1921, erected 1928, demolished 1950s–60s, restored 1989).

### A2-0656 sporto salė (gym), A2-1297 krepšinis (basketball), 138 komanda (optional)
- Place: Kaunas Sports Hall, Perkūno al. 5, engineer Anatolijus Rozenbliumas, architect S. Kudokas, built in six months 1938–39; the first hall in Europe built for basketball; venue of the 1939 European championship that Lithuania won.
- Look: a plain monumental box, 63 by 61 m, with four great riveted steel arches carrying a shallow vaulted roof with triangular skylights; austere plastered walls, portals with half-columns on two façades; inside, wooden stands around a parquet court, hoops on plain backboards, the arches visible overhead.
- 1981 note: still Kaunas's main hall in any 1981 (the real one was Žalgiris's home 1944–2010); in the counterfactual there is no Žalgiris lettering, just the tricolour.
- Sources: https://www.vle.lt/straipsnis/kauno-sporto-hale/ (dimensions, four 60 m arches, skylights, "Fasadai asketiški, monumentalūs", EuroBasket 21–28 May 1939); https://autc.lt/en/architecture-object/?id=791; https://www.vle.lt/straipsnis/lietuvos-krepsinis/ ("1939 Kaune pastatyta sporto halė – pirmasis Europoje statinys, skirtas krepšinio varžyboms").

### A2-0654 stadionas (stadium), A2-1260 futbolas, 106 žaidėjas / 107 sirgalius (optional swap from Vingis)
- Place: the State Stadium in Kaunas (today the Darius and Girėnas stadium), Ąžuolynas park; designed and built under Steponas Darius with Kęstutis Bulota 1923–25, enlarged 1935–36 as Valstybinis stadionas; hosted the 1938 National Olympics.
- Look (1981 counterfactual): an oval cinder running track around a grass pitch, low tiered stands with one covered wooden grandstand, tall oaks of the park behind, tricolour on a mast. The real 1969–79 Soviet rebuild (concrete half-ring) is not used.
- Why: the stadium itself is Darius's work, so the pilot card and the stadium card quietly rhyme.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darius_and_Gir%C4%97nas_Stadium (Darius and Bulota 1923–25, State Stadium 1935–36, 1938 Olympics, wooden stands replaced 1969–79); https://www.vle.lt/straipsnis/steponas-darius/ ("Parengė pirmojo Lietuvoje stadiono projektą ir vadovavo statybai").

### 048 ligoninė (hospital) — **flag, proposed swap**
- Existing: vilnius_antakalnis, "a long hospital block of the 1970s" beside Sts Peter and Paul. The block is Soviet-era; it is plausible but not Lithuanian-built.
- Proposed: Vytautas Magnus University Clinics, Kaunas (Eivenių g. 2), French architects Urbain Cassan and Elie Ouchanoff with F. Bielinskis, built 1937–39, opened 1940; six buildings over about 160,000 m², a 75 m chimney, underground corridors.

### A2-0653 kinas (cinema) — inset only
- Place: Romuva cinema, Laisvės al. 54, Kaunas, architect Nikolajus Mačiulskis, opened 13 April 1940; the oldest cinema still operating in Lithuania; UNESCO-listed.
- Look: a narrow modernist street front with a tall vertical fin rising above the roofline (its sign left blank), rounded canopy over the doors, poster frames left blank. Visual, unverified: the fin.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romuva_Cinema (1939–40, Mačiulskis, Art Deco/functionalism, European Heritage Label 2015).

### A2-0645 aikštė (town square)
- Place: Town Hall square, Kaunas Old Town, with the Town Hall ("White Swan"): begun 1542, baroque-classicist rebuild 1771–75, 53 m tiered tower, the tallest in the old town.
- Look: a wide paved square with a slender white town hall in the middle, its tower stepping up in ever-smaller tiers to a thin spire; low two- and three-storey old houses and a church gable around; benches, pigeons, a trolleybus along one edge (Kaunas trolleybuses from 1965 — unverified in the bank, keep or drop).
- 1981 note: the real building was a wedding palace from 1973; in the counterfactual a municipal office. Nothing visible changes.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaunas_Town_Hall (dates, 53 m tower, "White Swan", stands in the middle of Rotušės Square).

### A2-1293 koncertų salė, A2-0812 koncertas, A2-1747 orkestras
- Place: the Vilnius Philharmonic building, Vilnius Old Town (Aušros Vartų g.), built 1899–1902 as a theatre; the Great Seimas of Vilnius met there in 1905; the State Philharmonic has worked there since 1940.
- Look: a three-storey eclectic corner building with a rounded corner bay and tall arched windows on the upper floor; inside a white-and-gilt concert hall with a balcony and a wide stage. Visual, unverified: façade details and hall colours.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuanian_National_Philharmonic_Society (built 1899–1902, Great Seimas 1905, Philharmonic from 1940).

### A2-1282 kavinė (café)
- Place: Konrado kavinė, Laisvės al. 45, Kaunas: café since 1862, run by the Konradas brothers from 1920, the meeting place of interwar Kaunas writers and artists (Sruoga, Krėvė, Keliuotis, Cvirka, Truikys and others); renamed Tulpė after the war, closed 2003.
- Look: a two-storey brick house on the lime boulevard; inside, wall mirrors, small round tables, a gramophone, an upright piano, a plaster statue of a girl in a niche (1938–39 refit), a glass cake case.
- 1981 note: in a free Lithuania it keeps its name, but no lettering is drawn anyway.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konradas_Cafe.

### A2-1769 paminklas (monument) — **flag, proposed swap**
- Existing staging: "an interwar bronze of a seated poet in a frock coat with an open book on his knee" — this describes the Maironis monument in Kaunas, which is not interwar: it was made in 1977 by Gediminas Jokūbonis (Lenin Prize 1963). In a free 1981 it would not exist in that form.

Alternative: the Vytautas the Great monument (Kaunas, 1932, Vincas Grybas — not vetted here).
- Sources: https://www.vle.lt/straipsnis/gediminas-jokubonis/ ("Maironio Kaune, 1977"; "Lenino premija 1963"); https://www.vle.lt/straipsnis/juozas-zikaras/.

## C. Cards that stay generic (no fitting verified place)
- 029 oro uostas: Kaunas's interwar field was Aleksotas (a grass aerodrome with hangars); the real Kaunas airport at Karmėlava opened only in 1988 and Vilnius's 1954 terminal is Stalinist. Generic terminal; an inset of a small orange monoplane beside the hangars would tie it to the pilot card. Not verified in this pass.

- A2-0722 tramvajus — **flag:** Lithuania had no tram in 1981. The card can stay (the word exists), but the picture is of a foreign or counterfactual Klaipėda tram. Source: https://www.vle.lt/straipsnis/tramvajus/ ("Tramvajai Lietuvoje nuo 1904 važinėjo Klaipėdoje ... Per II pasaulinį karą įrenginiai sugriauti ir nebeatstatyti").

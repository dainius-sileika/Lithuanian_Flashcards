055 bankas: is the outside building historical?

The building it is meant to be is historical; the drawing does not show it. The card's settings row and staging place it in the Bank of Lithuania in Kaunas (Maironio g. 25 / K. Donelaičio g. 85; architect Mykolas Songaila; cornerstone 12 March 1925, opened 8 December 1928). The main picture is drawn as that bank's Operations Hall under its triangular skylight, which you called a good location. The outside inset was written loosely ("the bank's pale neoclassical corner front with its tall columned entrance") and the engine drew a generic portico: six columns under a flat entablature on a square corner.

The real building has a rounded corner at the crossing of the two streets, a granite base, and Kajetonas Sklėrius's sculpture group (allegories of Agriculture, Industry and Warfare) on the pediment above the attic on the Maironio street side. None of that is in the inset. No source I read describes a columned portico outside; the deck's own location entry already lists the façade's column order as unverified. So your courthouse reading is fair, and it matters here: 035 teismas is staged with the Kaunas Palace of Justice front in an inset, so a columned front on 055 points at the court card.

Sources: Money Museum, Bank of Lithuania buildings in Kaunas (rounded corner, granite, sculpture group); Money Museum e-guide (architect, dates, corner of Donelaičio and Maironio). I could not check a photograph of the façade from here.

Help choose the look of the Lithuanian deck

The deck's pictures are meant to look like pages from a 1981 Lithuanian civil-defence training manual, drawn in the style of Soviet Grazhdanskaya Oborona posters. We are choosing how the next thousand cards will be drawn.

There are 13 rounds. Each round shows 7 or 8 versions of the same card, in random order and unlabelled. For each round, drag the pictures into order from best to worst, tick Would ship on any you would happily see in the deck, and say what you like or dislike in the comment boxes. Go by your eye; there are no right answers. It takes about 20–30 minutes, and your progress is kept in this browser if you stop part-way.

At the end you'll get a short results file to send back to Dainius.

Round 1

Drag the pictures into order, best first (or use the arrows). Tick Would ship on every version you'd put in the deck as it is. Click a picture to see it larger.

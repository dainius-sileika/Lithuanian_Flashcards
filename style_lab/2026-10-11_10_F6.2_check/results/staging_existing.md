# Staging directions for the existing deck — approach and coverage

Answer to "all of them?": **no — about a third, chosen by risk, not by count.** 183 of 509 depictable cards get a line (`card_notes_existing_proposed.json`); the other ~325 are left to the class grammars and the adjective pair bases, which the 520 masters show already carry the word (animals in profile, vehicles, clothing flat-laid, foods, drinks, cutlery, tools, furniture, body parts, materials, nature, measures, shapes, colour clusters, the 60 adjective pairs, pronouns). Three reasons not to stage everything:
1. Every line adds 40–90 words after a 261-word lead and ~250 words of rules; on cards that already read correctly it can only dilute the print style or override a grammar that works (the food "cut view" clause is right for apples and wrong for soup; a blanket rewrite would have to re-derive 500 such cases tonight).
2. Under the current engine a card_notes entry suppresses the bank Setting line (`skip=set(CARD_NOTES)`); staging all cards would delete the "real Lithuanian place 2/3 of the time" the owner just asked for. The JSON carries a `_settings_policy` and `_settings_embedded` list so the merge can keep settings on everything except the ten notes that already name their place.
3. The failures seen so far (F5–F6.2 and the masters) cluster in four classes: places whose identity is an interior or an activity rather than a façade; people/role cards (gaze, duplicate faces, missing role props); abstract Society nouns; and verbs whose default insets or static pose misled. Staging those classes closes the gap; staging a cow does not.

## What was looked at
All 520 masters (`results/masters/m_00..12.jpg`), the owner's comments across the four blind rounds, VERB_STAGING / NOUN_STAGING / VERB_INSET / ADJ_PAIR in deck_builder (lines are written to agree with them: same setting, same dress class, same arrow, same emphasis; where NOUN_STAGING already has a "main" the note restates and extends it rather than replacing it), the class grammars, settings_assignments.csv (mode per card), and the F6.2 render.

## Tiers
**Tier 1 — masters that fail the two-second cover-the-word test (22 cards).** bilietas (blank slip → punched ticket in a conductor's hand), butas (block → one flat marked, balcony and interior), restoranas (small shopfront → dining room with waiter), kambarys (empty box read as "lift" → furnished room through its doorway plus a floor-plan inset), parduotuvė (→ counter, scale, shelves), biblioteka (building → reading room with shelves and card catalogue), bankas (columned façade = museum/court → cashier grille, safe, banknotes), žemė-ground (duplicated soil/spade of 304 → boots on the ground with a down arrow), vieta (→ finger on a plan at a marked spot), asmuo (→ one coloured individual in a row of silhouettes, arrow), advokatas (office desk → courtroom table with bench and scales), autorius (secretaries → typewriters, manuscript stack, shelf of the same book), taika (had the word PEACE → dove, blank banner with drawn symbol), raštelis, siena (plain wall read as "room" → wall face with palm, arrow, brick/plaster insets), sustoti (had STOP lettering → blank red disc), mirti (identical to mirtis → guttering candle and mourner distinguish it), tinklas, oras, stotis, sriuba (cut-open bowl), teatras (heraldic banner). Plus 304_žemė-soil sharpened so the pair ground/soil differ, 209 stalas, 227 raidė, 254 ekranas, 340 pusė, 361 vaizdas, 362 raštas small clarifications.
**Tier 2a — all other place/building cards (16).** Each line names the defining feature in the middle and says "… is the subject" so a bank Setting cannot redraw the type (the F6 mokykla/gatvė failure). Interiors are written as interiors (baras, biuras, teismas, restoranas, biblioteka, bankas); the Setting for these should be window/inset mode.
**Tier 2b — people and roles (40).** Gaze assignment per the pair rule, distinct faces, the role's defining prop and place, Sunday-best or role dress where the setting demands, Lithuanian touches where natural (kanklės, koplytstulpis, troba, rye bread, lilies of the valley, string bags, Ikarus, Zhiguli).
**Tier 2c — Society/abstract nouns (37).** The owner-confirmed 1.7.8 scenes kept; lines add the gaze, remove lettering risks (banknotes, ballots, certificates drawn "as plain shapes"), and give three concrete insets so the model stops inventing them.
**Tier 2d — verbs (47).** Only verbs on VERB_INSET_DEFAULT or with a static main (say, smile, laugh, cry, smell, taste, touch, kiss, stand, sleep, count, wash, cook, write, call, draw, catch, push, pull, carry, wear, hang, shake, sign, beat, lift, work, play ×2, eat, drink, kill, sit, lie, marry, clean, find, break, feed ×2) get the "moment of maximum legibility" plus three written insets (defining detail, second angle, result). Motion-strip and sequence verbs (eiti, bėgti, šokti-jump, go, throw, fall, bend, turn) and verbs whose VERB_STAGING already carries a consequence panel are left alone.

## Conflicts the merge must know about
- **430_maitinti**: the card_notes line in production (hens) contradicts VERB_STAGING 430 (mother spoon-feeding a baby; "feeding PEOPLE"), and the render followed VERB_STAGING, which is right since lesinti/šerti cover birds/livestock. Replaced with a people-feeding line. The wordlist subject phrase still says "scattering grain to chickens" — correct it in the next wordlist edit (not tonight).
- **053_žemė (ground)**: the subject phrase says "mound of dark earth with a spade", identical in substance to 304 soil. The note re-stages it as the ground underfoot; the phrase should become "the ground underfoot, a person standing on bare earth" in the next wordlist edit. Until then the note and MAIN_MEANING_RULE carry it; QA the card.
- **Interior place cards** (restoranas, biblioteka, bankas, parduotuvė, kambarys, baras, biuras, teismas): the place-class clause "Draw it from the front as a clear architectural elevation" is still appended after the note. gatvė showed the model follows the specific note, but a one-line deck_builder change is cleaner: when a card_notes line begins "Main picture:", drop the class main clause (keep the inset menu). Alternatively add `"main"` overrides in NOUN_STAGING for those eight rows.
- **Settings**: see `_settings_policy` in the JSON. Ten notes already embed their place (`_settings_embedded`); everything else should still receive the bank line, which should respect `mode` (interior → "Through the window: …", inset → "In one inset: …").
- **183_sriuba**: the food grammar's "cut or sliced view" is wrong for liquids; the note overrides it. Later, go_grammars `food.main` should branch on solid vs dish.
